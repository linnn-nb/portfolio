About me

Recording arts
and game audio.

I’m Hu JinLin, a Recording Arts undergraduate at Xinghai Conservatory of Music. I work across game audio, sound for picture and music production. The projects below show my sample editing, sound-to-picture work and interactive audio implementation.

My practical experience includes an audio design internship at Tencent IEG’s LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS and a music production internship at Taihe Music.

My dissertation proposal explores how granular synthesis and layered loops can be compared as approaches to vehicle engine sounds in games. I am interested in how synthesis and recorded material can be controlled by a player’s actions.
