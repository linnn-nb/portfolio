Sound design

I selected and edited the pencil and combat sounds, implemented the drawing controls in Unity and Wwise, and balanced them against music and combat feedback.

  • 00:29 · Drawing

    The friction loop follows movement. A turn adds a short accent; pausing the hand stops the loop.

  • 02:04 · Wave complete

macOS 12+ · Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Draw a route in the notebook, summon a creature and start the wave. Keep the application and its contents together after unzipping.

Roles & sources

Game design and visual art: Hua Shou team. Sound design, editing and interactive audio: Hu JinLin.

Pencil and paper textures: Office Building library via Splice. Sweep layers: Fantasy Game 2. I selected, edited, layered and implemented these library recordings.

Music includes Suno-generated material, selected, edited and integrated by me. The playable version uses a friction loop and turn accents.
