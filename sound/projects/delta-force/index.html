The project

Project & role

Overview

I used an existing promotional cinematic as a fixed picture edit and rebuilt its music and sound-effects track. The video below is the finished redesign.

My contribution

I edited the music and sound effects, synchronised them to the picture and made the final mix. The original film and visual edit are not my work.

Process

Sound breakdown

Synchronised action, environmental continuity and the rhythm of the edit.

01

Sync action to visible contact

Mechanical details and impacts are placed against the picture edit. Their attacks mark contact; their tails continue into the following frames.

02

Mix music against effects

The music spans the sequence. Close mechanical details and larger impacts move forward when their corresponding actions are visible.

Credits
